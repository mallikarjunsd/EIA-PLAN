PROJECT ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT PROPOSAL

Project Type:
Textile Manufacturing Plant

The proposed project is a textile manufacturing facility covering
approximately 120 acres of land.

The project will be located near a river and will consume approximately
2 million litres of freshwater per day.

The manufacturing process will generate approximately 500000 litres
of wastewater per day.

The facility will employ approximately 800 workers and will operate
continuously in three shifts.

The manufacturing process will use chemical dyes, solvents and other
hazardous materials.

The facility will contain dyeing, washing, processing and finishing
units.

Industrial machinery will operate continuously and may generate
significant noise.

A residential area is located approximately 2 km from the proposed
project site.

The region may experience heavy rainfall during the monsoon season.

The project will generate industrial waste, chemical waste and
packaging waste.

Chemical and hazardous materials will be stored within the facility.

The project will include an effluent treatment system, green-belt
development and emergency response infrastructure.
