PROJECT PROPOSAL

We are planning to establish a textile manufacturing plant on 120 acres of land near a river.

The plant will consume approximately 2 million litres of freshwater per day and generate approximately 500,000 litres of wastewater per day.

The project will employ around 800 workers and operate continuously in three shifts.

The manufacturing process will use chemical dyes, solvents and other hazardous materials.

The facility will include dyeing, washing, processing and finishing operations.

The project will generate industrial wastewater containing chemical residues and will require an effluent treatment system.

The plant will use industrial machinery continuously, which may generate significant noise.

A residential area is located approximately 2 km from the proposed project site.

The project site is located in an area that may experience heavy rainfall during the monsoon season.

The project will generate solid industrial waste, chemical waste and packaging waste.

The project will require storage areas for chemicals and hazardous materials.

The proposed project will include green-belt development around the facility and emergency response infrastructure.
